Common questions

How do I play? Draw every line of the figure in one stroke, without lifting your finger and without going over a line twice. Settings → How to play replays the tutorial.

The lines are too thin for my fingers. Settings → Finger size → Measure my finger, or pick Large / Extra large.

I bought Unlock Everything on another device. Settings → Restore purchase (same Apple Account).

How do I remove ads? The one-time Unlock Everything purchase removes all ads. No subscription.

What are talismans? Rewards for finishing each world, kept in the Treasury (seal button, top left of the title screen). Redeem one for a Frost Cache or keep it.

Ad privacy choices. In regions that require consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland), Settings → Privacy choices lets you change it any time. Everywhere: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
