Privacy Policy

Brain Freeze: Trace · Last updated November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you use Brain Freeze: Trace ("the app"). "We" means the app's developer.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or contacts. Your progress, stars, settings, talismans and finger-size setting are stored only on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) we ask for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown, and on iOS we ask for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice any time in the app's Settings → Privacy choices (where consent applies), or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

The "Unlock Everything" purchase is processed by Apple. We never see your payment details; Apple tells the app only whether the purchase is active.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are shared with Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data on our own servers, there is nothing for us to export or delete. Deleting the app removes all game data from your device. For data held by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy controls at myadcenter.google.com.

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes.
